A look inside

Good deals. Safe meetups.

  • Meet in public. A busy, well-lit place is best.
  • Check before you pay. Look the item over first, then pay with MoMo or cash.
  • No payments in the app. Havana never holds your money. Never send money in advance.
  • Your location stays private. Others see your area and a rough distance, never your exact spot.
  • Report anything off. Listings reported by several people are hidden and reviewed.
  • Ratings after you chat. Only people who actually traded words can rate each other.

Before your first find

A few things to know.

Can I download Havana today?

The public Android beta is opening soon. The download link will appear here when it is ready. An internal preview is being tested; it is not a Play Store release.

How do I pay for an item?

Arrange the sale with the seller in chat. Meet in public, inspect the item, then pay the seller directly with MoMo or cash. Havana does not process payments or hold money.

Do I have to swap, or can I just buy?

You can browse Shop and buy without offering anything in return. For swaps, add an item to your Swap Closet so the other person has something to choose.

Can I negotiate the price?

Yes. Open Haggle Mode to send an offer in chat. You can use the asking price or the suggested discounts, or enter an amount. The seller can accept, counter, or decline.

Where is Havana available?

Havana is built around Accra, with listings organized by areas such as Osu, Madina, East Legon, Labone, Spintex, and Kaneshie. Distance is approximate; agree on the actual meeting place in chat.

Will other people see my exact location?

Other users see your area and a rough distance rather than precise coordinates. You decide where to meet and what details to share in your conversation.

What phones does the Android beta support?

The preview requires Android 7 or newer and an internet connection. An iPhone download is not available yet. Availability updates will be posted on this page.

How do I report a listing or delete my account?

Report a listing from its detail page in the app. To remove your account, use “Delete account” in your profile or read the account deletion information. Our privacy policy explains how account data is handled.
