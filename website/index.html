A look inside

Good deals. Safe meetups.

  • Meet in public. A busy, well-lit place is best.
  • Check before you pay. Look the item over first, then pay with MoMo or cash.
  • No payments in the app. Havana never holds your money. Never send money in advance.
  • Your location stays private. Others see your area and a rough distance, never your exact spot.
  • Report anything off. Listings reported by several people are hidden and reviewed.
  • Ratings after you chat. Only people who actually traded words can rate each other.
